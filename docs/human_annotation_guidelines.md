# Independent sentiment intensity annotation

We are evaluating how accurately VADER identifies sentiment intensity in YouTube comments. Independently rate the overall sentiment expressed in each comment using only its text.

- -4: Extremely negative
- -3: Strongly negative
- -2: Moderately negative
- -1: Slightly negative
- 0: Neutral / neither positive nor negative
- +1: Slightly positive
- +2: Moderately positive
- +3: Strongly positive
- +4: Extremely positive

1. Evaluate the overall meaning of the comment.
2. Consider emojis as part of the sentiment.
3. Interpret common slang and abbreviations in context.
4. Consider sarcasm and irony where the intended meaning is reasonably clear.
5. Pay attention to negation.
6. Do not automatically classify short comments as neutral.
7. A question is not automatically neutral.
8. Do not judge whether you personally agree with the comment.
9. Do not use VADER, ChatGPT or other AI tools to determine your answer.
10. Do not discuss your ratings with the other annotator before both sessions are complete.

If sentiment cannot reasonably be determined from the text alone, use Skip for Now or Flag for Review. Do not force an arbitrary rating. You may leave a private note. Do not look up the video, images, creator or parent context. Missing ratings stay missing, rather than becoming zero.

Save & next commits your selection. Revisit saved ratings through Progress before finalisation. After finalisation, ask the researcher to reopen if a correction is necessary.
